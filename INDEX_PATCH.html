Risk prioritization and detection strategy for mission-critical infrastructure

Led a large-scale analytical initiative to identify technical vulnerabilities, human vulnerabilities, operational exposure points, and high-probability risk pathways across a mission-critical environment. The work combined stakeholder interviews, mission partner coordination, cyber risk analysis, intelligence-informed context, and metrics-based prioritization.

  • Coordinated with mission partners, cybersecurity stakeholders, operational teams, and intelligence personnel
  • Identified possible cyber vulnerabilities, human vulnerabilities, and operational exposure points
  • Designed a risk comparison framework to prioritize the most likely and highest-impact issues
  • Translated findings into safeguard recommendations and leadership-ready reporting
  • Recommended strategic network sensor placement to improve visibility into adversary movement

Analytical Methodology

Discovery Interviewed stakeholders and mission partners to understand systems, workflows, dependencies, and human factors.
Risk Identification Mapped technical vulnerabilities, operational exposures, and potential human-enabled risk pathways.
Risk Scoring Compared likelihood, potential impact, exposure, detectability, and operational consequence.
Safeguard Planning Translated ranked risks into recommended controls, process safeguards, and monitoring priorities.
Detection Strategy Recommended sensor placement and visibility points to better detect adversary movement.
