# Car Marketing AI Case Study

## Project Title

Car Marketing AI: Dealership Campaign Intelligence Platform

## Executive Summary

Car Marketing AI is a data-driven AI marketing assistant designed to help automotive dealerships decide which vehicles to promote, generate high-performing campaign content, identify target buyers, recommend optimal posting times, and rank inventory based on lead potential.

The key differentiator is that the system is not just a content generator. It combines decision-making, campaign automation, AI-generated messaging, rules-based analytics, vehicle scoring, and future performance tracking into one workflow.

## Product Vision

Help dealerships:

- Choose which vehicles to promote
- Generate campaign-ready social content
- Target the right buyers
- Post at optimal times
- Rank vehicles based on urgency, lead potential, and audience fit

## Business Problem

Dealerships often have large inventories and limited time to decide which vehicles should receive marketing focus. Marketing teams may manually review listings, write posts, choose audiences, and determine timing without a consistent scoring framework.

This project solves that problem by combining AI content generation with structured analytics and business rules.

## Core Features

### Vehicle Selection Interface

Users can select vehicles from inventory and filter by:

- Price range
- Days on lot
- Body type
- Mileage

### AI Vehicle Intelligence

The AI analyzes vehicle listings and extracts:

- Specs
- Key selling points
- Value positioning
- Why the car sells
- Who should buy it

### Audience Targeting Recommendations

For each vehicle, the system recommends:

- Primary audience
- Secondary audience
- Geo targeting suggestions
- Platform recommendations

### Smart Timing Engine

The system recommends:

- Best posting days
- Best posting times
- Platform-specific timing

### Content Generation

Generates multiple social media posts per vehicle using different campaign angles:

- Affordability
- Lifestyle
- Urgency
- CTA-driven messaging

### Promotion Ranking

Ranks selected vehicles by:

- Likelihood to generate leads
- Days-on-lot urgency
- Audience fit
